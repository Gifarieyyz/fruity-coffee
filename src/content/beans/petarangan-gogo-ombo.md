---
name: Petarangan Gogo Ombo
origin: Sumbing, Jawa Tengah
process: Hermetic Sealed Natural
roaster: AHEAD
notes: Green Grapes, Longan, Brown Sugar
---
The flavor has an incredible fruitiness that strongly reminds me of longan. The acidity is reminiscent of grapes (though not green ones), and it finishes with a sweet, lingering aftertaste. It is definitely worth trying. They don't appear to have an online store, so be sure to grab it if you ever get the chance!
